ingredients:
- marinade
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 5 sprigs cilantro
- 2 TBSP oil
- 2 TBSP soy sauce
- 1 TBSP fish sauce
- 1 TBSP sugar
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 TBSP Thai red curry paste
- Sweet Chili Sauce
- 3 TBSP sambal olek chili paste
- 2 cloves minced garlic
- 1/2 cup rice wine vinegar
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1 tsp salt

directions:
- Mix mariade
- toss with chicken (8 pieces roughly)
- set aside 1 hour or longer (best for 8-12 hours)
- bake @ 375F for 30in (boneless)
- crisp on cast iron skillet
- heat cause over med/low heat to gentle boil
- reduce heat & simmer 5 min
