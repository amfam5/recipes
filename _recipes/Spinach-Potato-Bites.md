ingredients:
- 1/2 sweet onion, finely diced
- 3 c finely diced potatoes
- 14 oz frozen chopped spinach 
- 6 eggs
- 1 Tbsp sea salt
- Black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1.5 cup potato flakes
- 2 Tbsp tapioca starch
- 4 Tbsp chia seeds


directions:
- Cover potatoes and onions with just enough water; boil, reduce heat and simmer until tender.
- In a separate bowl, combine potato flakes, tapioca starch, and chia seeds.
- Remove potatoes from heat and drain. Reserve one cup, then add spinach to pot and season.
- When cool, add eggs and puree.
- Stir in dry mix and reserved potatoes.
- Chill 30+ minutes (up to overnight)
- Preheat oven to 400, cover baking sheets with parchment (or oil mini muffin tins). 
- Drop by 1 oz scoop 
- Bake 45 minutes (35 for mini muffin tins)
- To freeze ahead, bake only 30-35 min. Thaw at room temp and reheat at 400 for 10 minutes.
