ingredients:
- 8 tablespoons (4 ounces) unsalted Irish butter (such as Kerrygold), divided

- 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped

- 1 medium head green cabbage (about 2 1/2 pounds), cut into 1/2-inch-thick shreds

- 1/4 cup water

- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt

- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more for garnish 

directions:
- Melt 4 tablespoons of the butter in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 1 minute. 


- Add the cabbage and water and stir until the cabbage is evenly coated with garlic butter. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage is just tender, 8 to 10 minutes. Reduce the heat as it cooks, if necessary, to prevent browning.


- Uncover, stir in the salt, pepper, and 2 tablespoons of the butter until melted, about 1 minute. Remove from heat; garnish with additional pepper and the remaining 2 tablespoon butter. Serve immediately.

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
