ingredients:
- 1/2 cup split red lentils
- 1 cup cold water

directions:
- For 6 wraps you'd soak half a cup of split red lentils and a cup of cold water and leave 6+ hours before blending to a really smooth paste. I just use my immersion hand blender in a jug. You can also use boiling water and wait around 30 minutes till cool, but I like the slightly fermented taste after 12 hours personally. I also like to add about half a tablespoon of olive oil to the blender as it makes them more pliable and they seem to stay softer longer.  
- You can also use stock instead of water or add spices, but I like the flexibility of something with barely any taste.  
- Once you have the 'batter', heat a lightly oiled pan till a drop of water sizzles, then pour 1/4 cup or 4 tablespoons of the batter into the middle and swirl in to a large circle with the back of a spoon working your way out till nice and thin(like you would a traditional dosa) .  
- Cook around a minute to a minute and a half on the first side till starting to get brown, turn over and the other side should take thirty seconds to a minute roughly.  
- You can leave wrapped in the kitchen for a day, or refrigerated for around 5 days, or freeze them for maybe 3 months. The batter also can be kept for around 3 days, so they can be fried off freshly as needed.  

---

These are amazing hot with something like spiced chickpeas or saag aloo etc or cold with anything you'd tend to usually put in a flour tortilla or sandwich.
