ingredients:
- 2 lbs chuck
- 1 TBSP oil
- 12 oz sliced mushrooms
- 2 small onions, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/4 tsp thyme
- 1.5 cup chicken stock
- 1 TBSP worchestershire sauce
- 1 TBSP soy sauce
- splash ACV
- 3-4 gold potatoes, chunked
- 2 mediu carrots, chuncked

directions:
- brown meat (wbed, or wbed after)
- saute mushrooms, reserve
- saute onions and garlic, deglaze
- add meat to liquids
- pressure cook 32min
- NR 10min
- pressure cook veggies 4 min. QR
- thicken liquid with starch or mash veggies
