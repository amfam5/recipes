ingredients:
- 3 lbs chuck roast
- 1 TBSP oil
- 2 small onions
- 1 dash wine or vinegar for deglaze
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 cup stock
- 1 TBSP soy sauce
- 1 TBSP fish sauce
- pinch rosemary
- pinch thyme
- 2 carrots
- 2-4 potatoes

directions:
- season and saute chuck in pan, 10min per side, then set aside
- saute 2-3 in
- deglaze
- return roast to pot. close lid & cook on high 45 min
- NR 25min
- remove roast & cover with foil
- submerge potatoes in liquid, stack carrots on top
- cook 4 minutes, QR
- Gravy - season to taste, then add 1 TBSP starch and saute until thicc
