 1. Classic Filipino BBQ Marinade

    ½ cup soy sauce

    ¼ cup calamansi or lemon juice

    ¼ cup banana ketchup

    2 tbsp brown sugar

    5 cloves garlic, minced

    1 tsp black pepper

    1 tsp salt

    1 tbsp oyster sauce (optional)

2. Korean Style

    ½ cup soy sauce

    ¼ cup honey or brown sugar

    1 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)

    4 cloves garlic, minced

    1 tbsp sesame oil

    1 tsp ginger, grated

    1 tbsp rice vinegar (or white vinegar)

3. Honey Garlic

    ½ cup soy sauce

    ¼ cup honey

    4 cloves garlic, minced

    1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce

    1 tbsp sesame oil

    1 tsp black pepper

4. Thai Style

    ½ cup fish sauce

    ¼ cup calamansi or lime juice

    1 tbsp palm sugar or brown sugar

    2 cloves garlic, minced

    1 tsp chili flakes

    1 tbsp cilantro, chopped

5. Miso Garlic

    1/4 cup miso paste

    1/4 cup soy sauce

    1/8 cup white sugar

    1 tbsp rice wine vinegar

    1 tsp ginger

    1/8 cup onion leeks

    3 cloves garlic
