ingredients:
- 3 cup vinegar (white or ACV) 5%
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp tumeric
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 2 tsp mustard seed
- 4 cup sugar
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- 4 lbs jalaneños, sliced

directions:
- prepare canner & jars
- combine all but jalaneños
- boil on high heat & disolve sugar
- add jalapeños
- reduce heat and simmer 15 minutes
- ladle into jars and leave 1/2" headspace
- remove air bubles and wipe rim clean
- lid & can, processing 15min


---

makes 4 pint jars
