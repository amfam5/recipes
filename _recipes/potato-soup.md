ingredients:
- 8 cups potatoes, diced
- 1 cup onion, finely diced
- 1 cup carrot, grated
- 1/4 cup butter
- sat & pepper to taste

directions:
- Cook onion in butter until tender
- Add potatoes and carrot, salt and pepper to taste
- Cover with water, filling just over the potatoes
- Simmer for 45 minutes
- Mash or immersion blend to thicken
