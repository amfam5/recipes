ingredients:
- 1/2 pt whipping cream
- jar with tight fitting lid

directions:
- fill jar 1/3-1/4 full
- screw on lid & shake, or roll across table/floor
- cream will thicken, then pieces of butter will start to form
- continue shaking/rolling until a solid mass of butter forms and separates from the buttermilk (10-15min)
