ingredients:
- 8.25 cup sliced cucumbers or 2.5 lbs spears
- 2 cup white vinegar
- 2 cup water
- 6 TBSP pickling salt
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 2 TBSP pickling spices
- 7 1/2 tsp dill seeds
- 5 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 1/4 tsp whole black peppercorns
- 5 cloves garlic, halved

directions:
- Stovetop in medium pan combine vinegar water, salt, sugar, spices
- Bring to boil
- Stir to dissolve
- Once dissolved, reduce and cover, boiling gently for 10 more minutes
- Pour liquid over slices/spears in heat-safe jar
- Cover & cool for 30 minutes
- in each jr place 1 1/2 tsp dill seeds, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1/4 tsp peppercorns, 2 garlic halves
- ladle liquid and cucumbers into jar
- leave 1/2 inch headpsace
