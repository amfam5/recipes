ingredients:
- 2 1/4 Lb cherry tomatoes 
- 1/4 c Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/4 c fresh basil (or 1 tsp dried) 
- 3/4 tsp salt 
- 1/2 tsp pepper 
- 1/4 c cream 
- 1 c broth 

directions:
- Toss the tomatoes with the garlic and oil 
- Bake in the oven for 35 min @ 425°
- Let cool 10 min 
- Blend all (strain skins)
- Add broth, basil, salt and pepper, cream 
- Blend until smooth
- Serve
