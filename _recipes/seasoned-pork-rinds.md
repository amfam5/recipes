ingredients:
- Store bought pork rinds 
- 2 tablespoons of seasoning mix 
- optional – oil

directions:
- Put 2 ounces of pork rinds into air fryer for 3 minutes at 350 degrees to get them warm and the oil to release. 
- Sprinkle seasoning. 
- Mix. 
- Enjoy.
