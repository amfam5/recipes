title:  "Dale's Gluten-Free Bread"
image: wait.jpg
imagecredit: self

tags:
- gluten-free

ingredients:
- 20gram psyllium husk
- 260gram water
- 130gram tapioca or arrowroot
- 100gram potato starch
- 170gram sorgum flour
- 15gram sugar
- 7gram xantham gum
- 4gram alum or cream of tartar
- 2gram baking soda
- 2gram starch
- 12gram sea salt
- 120gram milk
- 2 large eggs
- 50gram oil
- 60gram starter (or 30gram flour, 30gram water, 2tsp yeast)

directions:
- add water to psyllium & set aside
- combine dry
- add wet and mix
- ball
- proof 5-7 hours at room temp or overnight in fridge
- bake 40min @ 450degrees 30min @ 350degrees
