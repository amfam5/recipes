ingredients:
- I1 c yellow mustard 
- 1/2 c white Sugar 
- 1/3 c brown Sugar 
- 3/4 c apple cider Vinegar 
- 1/4 c water 
- 2 Tbsp smoked paprika 
- 1 tsp blk pepper 
- 1 tsp white pepper 
- 1 tsp soy sauce

directions:
- Mix and simmer on stove.
- Cool and serve.
- Save in jar for 2 weeks in fridge
