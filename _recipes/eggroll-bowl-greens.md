ingredients:
- 2 lbs ground pork
- 1/2 yellow onion
- 2-4 cloves garlic
- 24 bok choy leaves (or other greens)
- Salt
- White pepper
- Coconut aminos
- Opt sesame oil


directions:
- Season and brown pork (drain if necessary). Remove from pan or push to sides of wok to make room for veg.

- Saute onions until translucent. Add garlic and saute until fragrant. Add chopped bok choy stems and continue to cook until all is tender. Add small drizzle or spray sesame oil if needed. Mix with ground meat.

- Chop greens and add to pot. Sprinkle salt, white pepper, and drizzle coconut aminos (approximately 1/4 cup) over top. Steam and saute lightly over med-low heat until just wilted/beginning to darken/soften. Stir well and serve over rice or quinoa.
