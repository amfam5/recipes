ingredients:
- jar
- 3 whole vanilla beans
- 1 cup vodka or white rum

directions:
- prep beans by cutting into 1 inch pieces
- place alcohol in jar with beans in a 3 beans to 1 cup of alcohol ratio
- store in a cool dark place for 8 weeks (longer is better)
- when ready to bottle, use brown glass to prevent light entry
- add half a bean to each bottle so flavor can intensify


---

Makes 8 ounces

Note: May add 1 tsp dark spiced rum for flavor
