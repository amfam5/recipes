ingredients:
- baking soda
- citric acid
- 3 tsp liquid (water, milk, beer, etc.)
- 3 oz shredded cheese

directions:
- Mix up baking soda and citric acid dry in a 1.3:1.0 ratio. 
- Put 1/4 tsp of the above mixture into 3 tbsp liquid (water, milk, beer, etc.)
- Heat it up
- blend in about 3 oz shredded cheese of your choice
- Whisk it all up.
- More or less cheese depending on how runny you want it 

---
Sodium Citrate the secret ingredient to turn any cheese into the perfect smooth creamy nacho cheese, its formula is Na3C6H5O7 (NaCHO)
