ingredients:
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1.5 cup almond flour
- 0.5 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 1 cup total of blend of fruit, nuts, chocolate - mixings

directions:
- cream sugar & butter
- add remaining ingredients
- fold in mixings
- use large cookie scoop
- bake @ 350F for 16min
- let rest, better if let cool - they are too soft to eat when hot and need to firm up

---

For orange flavor add 1 TBSP orange zest (orange flavor goes well with these cookies)
