 1. Cilantro-Lime

    1/2 cup fresh cilantro

    1/2 cup lime juice

    1/8 cup olive oil

    1 tsp white pepper

    1 tsp cumin

    Salt and pepper to taste

2. Teriyaki

    1/4 cup soy sauce

    1/8 cup brown sugar

    4 cloves garlic, minced

    A thumb of ginger, julienned

    2 tbsps sesame oil

    1/4 cup cold water

    Salt and pepper to taste

3. Pinoy Inasal

    1/2 cup soy sauce

    1/4 cup vinegar

    8 pcs calamansi

    1 red onion, diced

    5 lemongrass o tanglad (stalk)

    2 tbsps brown sugar

4. Lemon Pepper

    1/4 cup lemon juice

    1/8 cup soy sauce

    4 cloves garlic, minced

    Salt and pepper to taste

5. Honey Garlic

    1/4 cup soy sauce

    4 cloves garlic, minced

    1 red onion, diced

    A thumb of ginger, julienned

    1 tbsp sesame oil

    1 tbsp honey

    Salt and pepper to taste
