ingredients:
- 1 cu white vinegar
- 1 cup water
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 tsps sugar
- 1 TBSP salt
- 7-8 jalapenños, sliced

directions:
- combine all except peppers, boil
- remve from heat and let sit 8 minutes
- add jalapeños and cover with liquid
- refridgerate


---

store in fridge for up to 2 months
