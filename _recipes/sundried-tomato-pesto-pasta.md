ingredients:
- 2 boxes gluten-free pasta (Banza)
- 6 to 7 ounces goat cheddar
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley
- 1/2 cup fresh basil
- 2 tbsp lemon dressin
- 2 tbsp water
- 8 sundried tomatoes, with EVOO
- 2 tsp salt

directions:
- Make pasta per box, reserve 1/4 cup water
- Make sauce with 1/4 cup water
- Mix with noodles
- Serve
