ingredients:
- 5 c dried fruit 
- 3/4 c brandy 
- 2 1/2 c Bob's GF flour (red bag) 
- 3/4 tsp xanthan gum 
- 1 TBSP cinnamon 
- 1/2 tsp mace 
- 1/2tsp cloves 
- 1 tsp baking powder 
- 1/2 c nuts 
- 1 c butter, softened 
- 1 c coconut Sugar

directions:
- Mix fruit w/ alcohol, let stand overnight 
- Preheat to 350° (325° for melal)
- Grease or line 6-8 mini loaf pans w/ parchment 
- Whisk together dry ingredients 
- cream together butter & Sugar 
- add eggs, one at a time. Beatsmath. 
- Add dry & Combine well. 
- fold in fruit & nuts 
- Scoop into pans & Bake 40-45 min 
- Turn oven down to 300°& bake 15 min. 
- cool & keep refrigerated.
