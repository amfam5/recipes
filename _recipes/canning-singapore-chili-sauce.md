ingredients:
- 2 1/2 cup vinegar (white or ACV) 5%
- 4 cup chopped fresh peppers (1lbs)
- 2 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 cup golden raisins
- 1/4 cup chopped garlic
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 2 tsp salt

directions:
- prepare canner & jars
- combine sugar and vinegar in steel saucepan
- boil on high heat & disolve sugar
- reduce heat and simmer 3 minutes
- add peppers and the rest
- bring to boil
- simmer 5 minutes
- ladle into hot jars and leave 1/2" headspace
- remove air bubles and wipe rim clean, lid finger tight
- return to canner, fill canner and cover with water til 1" above jar rims
- lid & can, processing 10min


---

makes 6 half pint jars
