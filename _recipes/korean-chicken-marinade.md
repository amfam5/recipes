ingredients:
- 1/2 c. soy sauce 
- 3-4 TBSP Goghingang 
- 1/4 c honey 
- 2 TBSP brown sugar 
- 2 TBSP sesame oil 
- 1 1/2 TBSP rice wine 
- 1 TBSP fresh ginger 
- 4 cloves minced garlic 
- reserve 1/2 c 
- marinate 4-8 hours 

directions:
- Mix
- reserve 1/2 c 
- marinate 4-8 hours
