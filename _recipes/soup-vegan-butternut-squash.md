ingredients:
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lrg onion
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 (3 lb) butternut squash
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp sage
- 1/2 tsp rosemary
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 3-4 c broth
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

directions:
- Saute onions and garlic in oil.
- Add everything else
- Cover and simmer on med-high until soft (20ish minutes)
- Blend
