ingredients:
- brussel sprouts
- sauce as desired

directions:
- Preheat your oven to 500°F (260 °C) with the empty sheet pan inside. In a large bowl, toss halved Brussels sprouts with  a liberal amount oil and salt until they're evenly coated; both help promote browning and temper bitterness. Then carefully add them to the hot pan. You should hear immediate sizzling. If you don't, the pan isn't hot enough.

- Arrange the sprouts cut-side down in a single layer and give them space. Crowding leads to steaming, and steaming is how Brussels sprouts lose all their appeal. Roast until the cut sides are deeply browned, and the outer leaves are crisp and shatteringly dry.

- From there, you can add whatever flavors you like—acids such as vinegar or citrus juice, cheese, chile sauce, or a glossy glaze—but the method stays the same - high heat, hard sear, no hesitation.
