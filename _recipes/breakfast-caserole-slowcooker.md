tags:

ingredients:
- 4 cups dried potato shreds, prepared (or 32 oz fresh or frozen)
- 1 lb breakfast sausage, browned (or other meat)
- 1 onion, diced and sauteed
- 1/2 cup salsa or diced peppers
- 2 cups kale (or other sturdy greens)
- 12 eggs

directions:
- Rehydrate potatoes and set aside.
- Brown breakfast meats and veggies, set aside.
- Season and whisk eggs. Add greens and stir.
- Grease/spray crock and layer 1/3 each potatoes and meat/veg mix, repeating.
- Pour over eggs/greens.
- Cook on low, 3 hours. Do not lift lid, steam will cook top of eggs and wilt greens properly if you leave it alone.
