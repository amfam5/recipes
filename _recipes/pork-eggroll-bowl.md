ingredients:
- 1 pound ground chicken or ground pork
- 4 green onions thinly sliced
- 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 16 ounces coleslaw mix or shredded cabbage - if cabbage, add 2 carrots grates and 1 thinly sliced onion
- 3 tablespoons soy sauce or coconut aminos
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil or to taste
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar or to taste
- 1 teaspoon white pepper
- chopped fresh cilantro for garnish
- sesame seeds for garnish


directions:
- Cook the meat til done
- Add cabbage
- Add seasoning
- Cook for like 2-3 min or until cabbage is softly crunchy, if it's wilted and soft you cooked too much
