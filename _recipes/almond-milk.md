ingredients:
- 2 c water, filtered 
- 1 1/2-2 Tbsp almond butter 
- 1 pinch salt 
- 2 Tbsp maple Symap, opt. 


directions:
- Blend on high 30 seconds to 1 min. until White & foaming. 
- Use or fridge for 2-3 days (shaking to re-mix)
