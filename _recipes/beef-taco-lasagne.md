ingredients:
- 8 oz cooked lasagne noodles
- 1 1lb beef
- 1 can refriend beans
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 lb jar salsa
- 4 cups cheddar
- 1/4 cup green onions
- sour cream
- olives, tomatoes, lettuce - taco toppings

directions:
- cook beef, add seasonings and beans
- layers should be salsa, noodles, beans/meat, cheese, salsa... repeat until out of ingredients
- cover and bake 350F 40min
