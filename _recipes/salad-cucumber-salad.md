ingredients:
- 1.5 lbs cucumbers, roughly 5 cups sliced
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 4-5 scallions, finely sliced
- 1-2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1/4 cup vinegar of choice
- 1 TBSP soy sauce/coconut aminos
- 1 TBSP sesame oil
- 1 TBSP honey/maple syrup
- 1 tsp chili garlic sauce
- 1-2 TBSP toasted sesame seeds

directions:
- Mix all together
- Let rest in fridge for 1-3 hours beore serving
